Thirty places and companies, forty connections

Every line on this graph exists because a named source says the two ends are connected: a contract, a shipment, a permit, a payslip, a stake. Where no source names both ends, there is no line, even when everyone knows the connection must exist.

In the chain view, material, hardware, compute and labour flow left to right into the AI companies. Only money (the dash-dot lines) flows the other way. In the hub view, the same forty rows are redrawn with the companies in the middle, the way an industry diagram would.

Node size is the number of connections. Select a node to see each connection and the source behind it.
