# AI Use Log – Make 4 (GIF & Remix Culture)

**Date:** September 30, 2026

**Tool Used:** Perplexity Computer (GPT Image 2.5 for the images; Perplexity for the GIF assembly script and the first draft of the page text)

**Task / Prompt:** Make one animated GIF, 3–10 seconds, that explores remix and repetition. The idea was to send one prompt to the image model several times with nothing added and loop the results, so the repetition itself shows what the model does on its own. First image prompt of the day: "Draw me as a hockey goalie making a save.", run five times (six outputs counting the one from my comic make). I then decided the make did not need to be about me and switched the subject. Final image prompt: "A golden retriever dancing in a living room.", run six times, no other instructions. I also asked Perplexity to sequence the six outputs into a loop with a prompt card at the start, a "try N of 6" counter, and a hold frame with red circles and a caption at the end, and to draft the process notes, reflection and attribution from my decisions.

**What the AI Suggested:** Goalie run: six near-identical pro goalies in an arena making the same glove save (not used). Dog run: six near-identical golden retrievers on their hind legs, one paw up, tongue out, in the same kind of bright living room with a gray couch, geometric pillows, cream rug, wood shelf and trailing plant, with a "Good Vibes Only" sign on the wall in three of the six and a mountain picture in the other three. It also chose a portrait format without being asked. Perplexity proposed the 0.4-second timing, the counter, the four labels on the last frame, the caption "Six tries. One dog. One living room. It only dances because I looped it.", the title, and a first draft of all the text.

**What I Accepted, Changed, or Rejected:** I dropped the goalie version and its five new outputs. I kept every dog image exactly as it came out, chose the order (ending on a frame with the sign so the hold frame could point at it), approved the timing, labels and caption after checking them against the actual frames (sign in tries 1, 2 and 6; mountain picture in 3, 4 and 5), and read and edited the drafted text before posting.

**Why:** The assignment asks for a visible human hand and honest disclosure, not polish. The machine made the pictures and the sameness. The human part is the experiment design, the choice to show all six instead of the best one, the order and timing that turn six stills into a dance, and the naming of what repeats.
