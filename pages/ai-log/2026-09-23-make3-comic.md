# AI Use Log – Make 3 (Comic)

**Date:** September 23, 2026

**Tool Used:** Perplexity Computer (GPT Image 2.5 for images; Perplexity for layout and text drafts)

**Task / Prompt:** Make a 5–6 panel comic that uses AI for at least one element. I started from the idea of continuing my selfie make with the part of my life I know best, goaltending. The first image prompt was exactly "Draw me as a hockey goalie making a save." with nothing added. The second was a description of the view from inside the mask at 6:30 a.m. practice. The panels of me at my desk were generated from a description of my selfie (curly hair, headphones, chain, green wall).

**What the AI Suggested:** A photorealistic pro goalie in a packed NHL arena for the first prompt; a through-the-cage view of an empty rink for the second; comic-style drawings of me for panels 1, 3 and 5. Perplexity also drafted the captions, speech bubbles, red markup labels and the commentary, and assembled the layout.

**What I Accepted, Changed, or Rejected:** I kept the first AI image exactly as it came out, because the point of panel 2 is what the AI does by default. I chose which assumptions to circle in panel 4 based on what I actually know about the position, and what the second prompt should ask for. I edited the drafted text before posting.

**Why:** The assignment asks for visible human intervention and honest disclosure. The AI made the pictures and a first draft of the words; the story, the corrections and the final say were mine.
