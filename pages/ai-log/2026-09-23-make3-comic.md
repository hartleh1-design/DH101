# AI Use Log – Make 3 (Comic)

**Date:** September 23–27, 2026

**Tool Used:** Perplexity Computer (GPT Image 2.5 for images; Perplexity for research, layout and text drafts)

**Task / Prompt:** Make a 5–6 panel comic that uses AI for at least one element. I chose a version of me that doesn't exist yet: a pilot. The first image prompt was exactly "Draw me as a pilot." with nothing added. The second described the view from the left seat of a small trainer at 6:30 a.m. (checklist on my knee, headset on the dash, instructor's hand by the other yoke). The panels of me at my desk were generated from a description of my selfie (curly hair, headphones, chain, green wall).

**What the AI Suggested:** For the first prompt, a photorealistic selfie of a smiling airline captain with four stripes in a jet cockpit, a completely different person from me. For the second, a worn small-plane cockpit at dawn. Comic-style drawings of me for panels 1, 3 and 5. Perplexity also looked up the aviation facts behind the panel 4 markups, drafted the captions, speech bubbles, labels and commentary, and assembled the layout.

**What I Accepted, Changed, or Rejected:** I kept the first AI image exactly as it came out, because the point of panel 2 is what the AI does by default. I decided the story should be about not being able to check the second image, since I have never flown, and kept that image even though I can't grade it. I read and approved the drafted text before posting.

**Why:** The assignment asks for visible human intervention and honest disclosure. Here the honest thing to say is that the AI made the pictures, did the fact-finding, and drafted the words, and that on a subject I don't know, my main contribution was noticing the limits of my own judgment and building the comic around that.
